Support (English)

Match Voyage is a match-3 puzzle game for iOS. If you run into any problem or have a question, this page is the place to start.

Contact us: wanggyxfmail@gmail.com
We usually reply within 48 hours.

1. Before you email us — quick fixes

  • Game won't start or crashes: make sure iOS is up to date (Settings → General → Software Update), restart the device, then update or reinstall the App from the App Store.
  • Sound / music issues: check the in-game settings screen, and make sure the device is not in Silent mode.
  • Leaderboards / achievements not working: these require Game Center. Sign in at Settings → Game Center, then relaunch the App.
  • Advertising: the current release does not display ads or include an advertising SDK.

2. Game progress

Your progress, settings and profile picture are stored only on your device — there is no account and no cloud save. Please note that deleting the App also deletes your progress. We cannot restore local progress from our side.

3. Purchases & subscriptions

All purchases are processed by Apple. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the in-game store. Manage or cancel a subscription via Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. For billing questions (refunds, charge errors) contact Apple Support, as we never see your payment details.

4. Reporting a bug

To help us fix things fast, please include:

  • Device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • App version (shown on the in-game settings screen / App Store page).
  • What you were doing when the problem happened, and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.

5. Feedback & feature requests

Ideas are welcome at the same email address — we read everything.

技术支持（中文）

Match Voyage 是一款 iOS 三消益智游戏。遇到问题或有疑问时，请先查看本页。

联系邮箱：wanggyxfmail@gmail.com
我们通常会在 48 小时内回复。

1. 发邮件之前——快速自查

  • 无法启动或闪退：请将 iOS 更新至最新版本（设置 → 通用 → 软件更新），重启设备，然后在 App Store 中更新或重新安装本应用。
  • 声音/音乐问题：请检查游戏内设置页，并确认设备未处于静音模式。
  • 排行榜/成就不可用：这些功能依赖 Game Center。请在 设置 → Game Center 中登录后重启游戏。
  • 广告：当前版本不展示广告，也不包含广告 SDK。

2. 游戏进度

你的进度、设置与头像仅保存在本机——没有账号、没有云存档。请注意：删除本应用会同时删除进度，我们无法从服务器端恢复本地进度。

3. 购买与订阅

所有购买均由 Apple 处理。换新设备后请在游戏内商店使用恢复购买。管理或取消订阅请前往 设置 → [你的姓名] → 订阅。退款、扣费等账单问题请联系 Apple 支持，我们不会看到你的付款信息。

4. 反馈 Bug

为帮助我们快速定位问题，请附上：

  • 设备型号与 iOS 版本（设置 → 通用 → 关于本机）。
  • 应用版本（游戏内设置页 / App Store 页面可见）。
  • 问题发生时的操作步骤与预期表现。
  • 如有可能，请附截图或录屏。

5. 建议与功能请求

欢迎通过同一邮箱提出建议，我们都会阅读。
