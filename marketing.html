How it plays核心玩法

Easy to learn, hard to put down. One goal per level: reach the target score before you run out of moves.
每关目标：在步数用完前达到目标分数。

💎Match & Combo · 消除与连击

Swap two adjacent gems to match 3+. Chain cascades for COMBO multipliers.
交换相邻宝石凑成三消，连锁消除触发连击加成。

🌈Special Gems · 特殊宝石

Match 4 for a Line clearer, 5 for a Rainbow, an L/T shape for a Wrapped gem that blows up a 5×5 zone.
四连成直线、五连成彩虹、L/T 形生成包裹宝石，引爆 5×5 范围。

🧊Blockers & Bosses · 障碍与首领关

Chip through jelly, ice and chains; boss levels ask you to clear a target color instead of a score.
果冻、冰块、锁链层层破解；首领关需消除指定颜色。

🔨Power-ups · 道具

Hammer, Bomb, Shuffle, Color Bomb, +5 Moves — turn a tight round around.
锤子、炸弹、洗牌、彩色炸弹、加五步——逆风翻盘。

A journey around the world环游世界

A 100-level campaign across six regions — China, Japan, Egypt, Europe, the Americas and Oceania. Every landmark you clear is stamped into your personal Passport, ready to share with friends.
六大区域（中国、日本、埃及、欧洲、美洲、大洋洲）共 100 个关卡。每抵达一个地标，你的专属护照就会盖上一枚印章，并可分享给好友。

  • Battle Pass · 战斗通行证 — earn XP from stars and wins, unlock tier rewards all season. 星星与胜利累积经验，整季解锁等级奖励。

  • Hearts & coins · 生命与金币 — a fair free-to-play loop: hearts refill over time, coins buy power-ups. 公平的免费游玩循环：生命随时间恢复，金币购买道具。
  • 9 languages & dark/light themes · 9 种语言与深浅主题 — play in your language, your way. 支持中、英、日、韩、法、德、西、葡、阿拉伯语。
