Privacy Policy (English)

Gem Match ("the App") is a match-3 puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its third-party service providers may collect, and how it is used. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. We have no server — we don't collect your data

The App has no backend server operated by us. The developer does not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal information to our own systems. Your game progress, settings, and profile picture are stored locally on your device only.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may independently collect data:

  • Advertising — Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The App shows ads served by Google. Google and its partners may collect and process: device information (model, operating system, IP address, approximate location), advertising identifiers (such as the Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID, where available), and ad interaction / usage data, in order to serve and measure ads (personalized or non-personalized). This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's advertising settings, Apple's App Tracking Transparency / "Limit Ad Tracking" setting, or Google Ads Settings.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions — Apple. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We never see or store your payment or card details. Apple retains your purchase records under Apple's Privacy Policy. The App keeps only a local record of the items you have unlocked.
  • Game Center — Apple (optional). If you sign in with Game Center, Apple collects your scores, achievements, and leaderboard activity to provide those features, under Apple's Privacy Policy. This is optional and controlled by you.

3. Permissions the App requests

  • Camera & Photo Library: used only so you can set a custom profile picture. Any photo you take or choose is stored on your device and is never uploaded to a server.

4. Data storage & deletion

All App data (progress, settings, a local anonymous identifier, and your avatar) is stored locally on your device using iOS local storage. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will promptly delete it. Note that third-party ad serving is configured to respect platform child-safety settings.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Opt out of personalized ads via device / Google ad settings (see Section 2).
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the App.
  • Contact us to exercise any privacy right (access, correction, deletion).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page; the "Effective date" above will be updated.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: wanggyxfmail@gmail.com

隐私政策（中文）

Gem Match（下称"本应用"）是一款三消益智游戏。本隐私政策说明本应用及其第三方服务商可能收集哪些信息、如何使用。使用本应用即表示你同意本政策。

1. 我们没有服务器——不收集你的数据

本应用没有任何由开发者运营的后台服务器。开发者不会收集、存储、出售或传输你的个人信息到自有系统。你的游戏进度、设置和头像仅保存在你的设备本地。

2. 第三方服务收集的信息

本应用使用了以下第三方服务，它们可能独立收集数据：

  • 广告 — Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）。本应用展示由 Google 提供的广告。Google 及其合作伙伴可能收集和处理：设备信息（型号、操作系统、IP 地址、大致位置）、广告标识符（如 Apple IDFA 或 Google 广告 ID，在可用时）、以及广告互动/使用数据，用于投放和衡量广告（个性化或非个性化）。该收集行为受 Google 隐私政策约束。你可以通过设备的广告设置、Apple 的"App 跟踪透明度/限制广告跟踪"，或 Google 广告设置关闭个性化广告。
  • 应用内购买与订阅 — Apple。所有购买完全由 Apple 的 App Store / StoreKit 处理。我们绝不会看到或存储你的付款或银行卡信息。Apple 依据 Apple 隐私政策保存你的购买记录。本应用仅在本地保存你已解锁项目的记录。
  • Game Center — Apple（可选）。如果你登录 Game Center，Apple 会依据其隐私政策收集你的分数、成就和排行榜数据以提供相应功能。此为可选项，由你自行控制。

3. 本应用请求的权限

  • 相机与相册：仅用于让你设置自定义头像。你拍摄或选择的照片保存在设备本地，绝不会上传到任何服务器。

4. 数据存储与删除

本应用的全部数据（进度、设置、本地匿名标识、头像）均通过 iOS 本地存储保存在你的设备上。卸载本应用将永久删除这些数据。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下（或你所在国家/地区的最低年龄）的儿童，我们也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童通过本应用提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们将立即删除。第三方广告投放已配置为遵守平台的儿童安全设置。

6. 你的选择与权利

  • 通过设备/Google 广告设置关闭个性化广告（见第 2 节）。
  • 管理或取消订阅：设置 → [你的姓名] → 订阅。
  • 卸载本应用即可删除全部本地数据。
  • 联系我们以行使任何隐私权利（访问、更正、删除）。

7. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。变更自发布到本页面时生效，上方"生效日期"将相应更新。

8. 联系我们
